# People handbook

## Annual leave
Employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Request annual leave in PeopleHub at least two weeks before your first day away. Your manager approves annual leave requests.

## Sick leave
Report sick leave to your manager before 10:00 on your first day of absence. Record sick leave in PeopleHub. A medical certificate is required after three consecutive working days.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of paid parental leave. Contact the People team eight weeks before the planned start date.

## Onboarding buddy
Every new employee is assigned an onboarding buddy for their first 30 days. The onboarding buddy helps with tools, introductions, and team routines.

## Learning budget
The annual learning budget is 1200 EUR per employee. Courses, books, and conferences are eligible for the learning budget. Obtain manager approval before purchasing learning materials.

## Performance reviews
Performance reviews happen twice a year, in March and September. Employees submit a self-assessment before each performance review.
