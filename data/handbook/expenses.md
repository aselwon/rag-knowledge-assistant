# Travel and expenses FAQ

## Expense deadline
Submit expense claims within 30 days of purchase through ExpenseFlow. Every expense claim must include an itemized receipt and business purpose.

## Travel booking
Book business travel through TravelDesk after manager approval. Economy class is required for flights shorter than six hours.

## Meal allowance
The business travel meal allowance is 45 EUR per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable under the meal allowance.

## Expense payment
Approved expense claims are paid in the next monthly payroll. Questions about expense payment should be sent to finance@example.test.

## Office visitors
Register office visitors with reception at least one working day in advance. Visitors must wear a badge and remain accompanied in secure areas.
