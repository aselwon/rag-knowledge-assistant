# Working together

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely within their country of employment. Remote work from another country requires written approval from People and Legal before travel.

## Core collaboration hours
Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 Central European Time. Schedule team meetings within core collaboration hours whenever possible.

## Home office equipment
New employees receive a home office equipment budget of 500 EUR. Eligible home office equipment includes a desk, chair, monitor, and keyboard. Submit receipts through ExpenseFlow.

## Internet reimbursement
Home internet reimbursement is capped at 30 EUR per month. Submit your internet bill through ExpenseFlow by the fifth day of the following month.

## Meeting notes
Every project decision must be recorded in written meeting notes in the team workspace. Meeting notes should include the decision owner and next steps.
